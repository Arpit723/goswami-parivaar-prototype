How Family Linking Works

Goswami Parivaar · one-page explainer for the team
You only ever confirm 3 kinds of links — parent, spouse, child.
Every other relative — dada, kaka, mama, cousin — the app calculates automatically.

Why "Parekh" finds "Parikh"

every name gets a sound-code
Rule
Parekh→prk
Parikh→prk
Shri Rameshbhai→rmshtitles dropped
How the code is made
1 · Drop titles (Shri, Smt, Bhai, Ben)
2 · Merge similar sounds — ph→f, w→v, ee→i, ck→k, th→t
3 · Remove vowels after the first letter
4 · Collapse doubled letters
Same sound-code ⇒ treated as the same surname ⇒ candidate found ✔

The three safety rules

the only rules that matter
Two Yes votes, always

No link is ever created from one side. Pending → Confirmed needs both people to tap yes.

Wrong guesses are remembered

"Not him" is stored — that person is never suggested to you for that relation again.

Fights go to a human

Two claims on the same person → Disputed → the city Sub-Admin decides. The app never judges family disputes.
