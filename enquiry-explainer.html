How Enquiry Works

Goswami Parivaar · Marketplace · one-page field & screen guide
An enquiry is a letter, not a chat. The buyer writes once, both sides choose independently whether to share their phone, the reply lands in the buyer's Inbox — and the deal settles outside the app.

3

Who sees whose phone?

four outcomes — two independent switches
Both ON
📞 + 📞
Buyer's number visible to business · business number travels in the reply. Fastest path — just call.
Buyer ON · Business OFF
📞 → 📵
Business can call the buyer; buyer reaches business only via Inbox replies.
Buyer OFF · Business ON
📵 → 📞
Business gets no number; its reply letter includes its own number for the buyer.
Both OFF
📵 ⇄ 📵
Full letters-only exchange inside the app. Perfectly fine — many deals close this way.
"Per exchange" means every enquiry starts fresh. Sharing on one enquiry never carries to the next. Blocked users see a generic "not available" — the block is never disclosed.
4

The life of an enquiry

three states, strict rules
NEW — waiting for the business → REPLIED — answer in buyer's Inbox → CLOSED — archived (either side)
NEW
Buyer waiting. Reply unlocks REPLIED. Buyer may close or withdraw. Auto-merges duplicates.
REPLIED
Reply is immutable — no editing after send. Buyer's phone decision already locked. Review now becomes possible.
CLOSED
Either side closes. Moves to Archived. Kept 12 months. A closed thread still qualifies the buyer to review.
Review eligibility: an enquiry that reached REPLIED or CLOSED — no drive-by ratings (EC-R-01)
