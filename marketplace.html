Admin UI

admins & Sub-Admins (city-scoped) · in-app module, 2FA login · server-enforced RBAC

Nothing is public without approval: new listings and edits pass the admin queue (approve / request change / hold — no rejections), reported reviews get evidence-based decisions, categories are managed live, and every action lands in the immutable audit log. The console is a role-gated section of the SAME app on iOS, Android and Web — unlocked after 2FA login; Sub-Admins see city-scoped queues.

Ratings & Reviews

earned trust · any verified member may review · moderated

Reviews are the module's memory: 1–5 stars + text, one per member per product (and per business), editable, with one business response that can never alter the review. Reported reviews leave public view and go to the admin queue; hidden reviews drop from the average automatically.

MP-030 · Reviewssummary · histogram · response · write sheet
MP-031 · Moderationreported → pending → decision → notice
Review rules: eligibility = any verified member · 1 review per member per target (product · business · individual provider), editable · no contact details (validated) · report → hidden pending admin · hidden = excluded from average · author always told why.
